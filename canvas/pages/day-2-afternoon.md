## Exercise 1 — readable history, storage, and access

For **your** study, choose how you will keep a readable history of working documents, where research data will be stored and backed up, and who may access the data. 

**Default for shared text:** one master in **Office Online** on a KI Teams/SharePoint project site; download into the project folder regularly; name exports per KI §6.1. Google Docs or personal OneDrive are fine for shared text if your group prefers them. If you are not co-writing online, dated copies plus a decision log still work. Git is fine if you already use it.

Restricted research data belongs on [KI-approved storage](https://staff.ki.se/research-support/research-data-management/create-collect-store-research-data).

**Do:**

1. **Alone first (≈25–30 min).** For *your* study, draft in the SRC template:
   - Under **Documentation and data quality** — *How will the material be documented and described, with associated metadata…?* Update your Day 1 answer with how you will keep a readable history (Office Online + named downloads, dated copies + decision log, or Git if you already use it), including folder structure and version control during the project.
   - Under **Storage and backup** — *How is storage and backup of data and metadata safeguarded during the research process?* Where will the research data and its metadata live, and how are they backed up?
   - Under **Storage and backup** — *How is data security and controlled access to data safeguarded, in relation to the handling of sensitive data and personal data, for example?* Who may open the data? What must stay inside the research team?
2. **Then in your TBL team (≈15–20 min).** Each person briefly states their choices. For each project, ask: can this person get back an earlier working document? Is storage of the research data clear? Is access clear? Discuss where you got stuck or disagreed.
3. As a team, post **2–3 unclear points** on the shared board. One short line each is enough (for example: “Unclear whether interview transcripts can sit in the shared Office Online master”). 
4. Join the **whole-group discussion** of the board posts (plan for a longer share-out than Exercise 2).

## Exercise 2 — refine the folder, metadata, and responsibilities

Update the project folder and README so a classmate could find their way around. Decide what metadata you will keep, how you will document data quality, and who is responsible for data management.

**Do:**

1. **Alone (≈10–15 min).** For *your* study:
   - Refine the folder, README, and decision log (naming, pointers to material on approved storage, what changed since Day 1).
   - Draft in the SRC template:
     - Under **Documentation and data quality** — *How will data quality be safeguarded and documented (for example repeated measurements, validation of data input, etc.)?* Include what metadata you will keep (README overview, data dictionary or codebook, naming, access conditions) and one quality check that fits your design.
     - Under **Responsibility and resources** — *Who is responsible for data management and (possibly) supports the work with this while the research project is in progress? Who is responsible for data management, ongoing management and long-term storage after the research project has ended?*
2. **Within your team — full [5-year rubric](five-year-rubric.md).** Brief pass: each person shows folder, README, decision log, and today’s DMP drafts (about one minute). Discuss the rubric questions.
3. As a team, post **2–3 findings** on the shared board.
4. Join the **whole-group discussion** of the board posts.
