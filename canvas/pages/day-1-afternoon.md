# Day 1 afternoon — Team-Based Learning

**Time:** 13:00–16:00  
**Venue:** room **Future** + [Zoom](https://ki-se.zoom.us/j/68142022512)

Bring the [SRC DMP template](../files/Swedish_Research_Council_Template__v5.docx). You will work on a project folder for **one study in your PhD**; Day 2 continues with that same folder. Incomplete drafts are fine.

We begin with an individual/team question round on the morning’s material. Then you will compare example project folders and work on your own study’s documentation and DMP draft.

Every exercise ends the same way: your team posts **2–3 findings** on the shared board, then the whole group discusses those posts.

## Exercise 1 — compare two project folders

Your team opens two synthetic project folders for the same fictional bednet study ([Exercise 1 on Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1vQgA7VL_Bw2BN6U4PwI0Mk94ZPkJ2hpG)):

- `bednet-survey-transnzoia`
- `bednet-study`

Use the [5-year rubric](five-year-rubric.md) as your guide (all questions).

**Do:**

1. Compare the folders with the rubric questions. For each difference that matters, also ask: does this help or block someone recreating the work from the same data and analysis ([Turing Way](https://book.the-turing-way.org/reproducible-research/overview/overview-definitions/)), or following how interpretations were reached ([auditability](https://www.keele.ac.uk/korsa/qualitative/reproducibilityreplication/))?
2. As a team, agree on **2–3 findings** and post them on the shared board. Name the folder and which rubric question it answers.
3. Join the **whole-group discussion** of the board posts. Be ready to say why, not only what looked tidy.

## Exercise 2 — your project folder and DMP draft

Create or improve a project folder for one study in **your** PhD project. Add or update a short README explaining the study and how its files are organised. Keep the 5-year test in mind from Exercise 1, but you do not need to walk the full rubric again here.

**Do:**

1. Create or improve the folder and README (manual drafting is fine).
2. If you use AI: prefer Microsoft Copilot because it is supported by KI; never put personal, patient, or sensitive research data in prompts; keep the prompt and the output; verify and edit; disclose use to your team.
3. Use your folder and documentation work to draft answers under **data description** (both prompts) and **documentation** in the [DMP template](../files/Swedish_Research_Council_Template__v5.docx). Mark the text as *draft*.
4. **Within your team (light check).** Take turns briefly showing your folder, README, and DMP drafts (about 2–3 minutes each). Could a colleague find their way around the project? What would make it easier to understand? Note **one** improvement to make before Day 2.
5. As a team, post **2–3 findings** on the shared board — patterns or stuck points from the check (for example: “Three of us had no place for exclusions yet”). No personal or patient detail.
6. Join the **whole-group discussion** of the board posts.

## Rules for the room

- Keep personal and patient data off shared screens and out of AI prompts.
