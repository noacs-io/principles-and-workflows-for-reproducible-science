# 5-year rubric

Use this as a **discussion guide**, not a grade sheet. Do not score people.

**The 5-year test:** someone returning in five years (you, a colleague, or a tool) can understand what was done and which decisions were made.

**When to use it:** Day 1 Application 1 (comparing the two synthetic folders), and the peer critique at the end of Day 2. Other activities do not require a full walk through these questions.

For each question: point at a file, folder, or sentence, or say what is still missing. Teammates may ask clarifying questions. No pass/fail.

| Criterion | Ask |
|-----------|-----|
| Orientability | From the README alone, what is this project, who is involved, and where do I start? |
| Separation of concerns | Which files are raw data, which are derived, and which are docs or procedures? |
| Decision traceability | Where would I find why a cleaning step, exclusion, or method choice was made? |
| Naming and versions | If I sort the folder by name, is the latest version obvious? |
| Ethics and access | What may be shared, what must stay protected, and where does the official record live (ELN / DMP)? |
| Handover readiness | Could a new colleague restart next week without calling you? |
| AI transparency | If AI was used, where is that disclosed, and what did a human change? |
