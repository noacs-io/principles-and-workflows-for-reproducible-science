# 5-year rubric

Use this when you compare folders, build your own package, or critique a teammate’s work.

**The 5-year test:** someone returning in five years (you, a colleague, or a tool) can understand what was done and which decisions were made.

| Criterion | Look for |
|-----------|----------|
| Orientability | Clear README: purpose, people, status, how to navigate |
| Separation of concerns | Raw data / derived outputs / code-or-procedures / docs are distinct |
| Decision traceability | Major choices dated and justified (analysis, cleaning, exclusions, tool versions) |
| Naming and versions | Consistent, sortable names; latest version obvious |
| Ethics and access | Explicit notes on personal data, sharing limits, where the official record lives (ELN / DMP) |
| Handover readiness | A new colleague could restart without relying on oral history |
| AI transparency | AI assistance disclosed and verified (when AI was used) |

Score against the criteria. You do not need a number for every row; say what passes, what fails, and why.
